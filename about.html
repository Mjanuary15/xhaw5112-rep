ABOUT US

Gaming that brings people together.

Discover the story, mission and goals behind Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena.

History

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo. The arena was created to give gamers access to high-performance equipment and organised competitive events.

Vision

To become a welcoming and modern gaming destination where casual and competitive gamers can play, compete and connect.

Mission

To provide quality gaming experiences, esports activities and memorable events for individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs and businesses.

Goals

  • Make gaming experiences accessible to a wider audience.
  • Provide a simple online booking and quotation process.
  • Support competitive esports and community events.
  • Create a modern, friendly and exciting gaming environment.
